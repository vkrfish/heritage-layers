# Heritage Layers

9 STORIES & CO.

ROLE

Act as a world-class multidisciplinary digital product designer, UI/UX designer, brand designer, art director, conversion-rate optimization specialist, and premium food e-commerce designer.

Design and build a sophisticated, premium e-commerce website for:

Brand: 9 Stories & Co.
Product: Authentic handcrafted Putharekulu
Category: Premium Indian sweets / Andhra heritage food / gifting
Positioning: Andhra heritage × handmade craftsmanship × contemporary luxury × premium gifting

The website must feel like a premium heritage food brand rather than a generic Indian sweets store.

1. BRAND STORY

9 Stories & Co. celebrates authentic Indian sweets through craftsmanship, heritage and storytelling.

The hero product is Putharekulu, the delicate Andhra sweet traditionally made using extremely thin rice starch sheets, ghee and sugar.

The website should communicate:

Authenticity

Heritage

Handmade craftsmanship

Premium quality

Cultural pride

Gifting

Trust

Elegance

Freshness

The emotional reaction should be:

"This feels authentic, premium and beautifully crafted."

2. BRAND VISUAL LANGUAGE

Use the visual concept:

ANDHRA HERITAGE × CONTEMPORARY LUXURY

Traditional craftsmanship
+
Editorial luxury
+
Modern e-commerce
+
Premium gifting
+
Authentic Indian identity

The website should make the visitor want to:

See the product → Understand the craft → Trust the brand → Buy → Gift

The logo's original Deep Teal, Orange and Aqua identity must remain recognizable, but the website should introduce Warm Cream, Parchment, Brown and restrained Gold to make the brand feel warmer, more premium and more appropriate for a heritage food business.

Do not redesign the logo.
